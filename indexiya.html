Our story

Roasted in-house, served with intention.

Iti Hasa began with a simple belief — that a good cafe is less about the coffee and more about the pause it gives you. So we built a roastery in the back, a pastry bench by the window, and a room that invites you to stay a while.

Every bean is roasted within sight of the bar, every pastry laminated by hand the night before. Nothing arrives in a box; everything arrives with a story.
